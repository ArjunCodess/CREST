# Research draft outline (evidence-backed only)

This is not a finished conference paper. It summarizes questions the V1 pilot can support with synthetic artifacts in `results/research/`.

1. **Problem:** Under missing mainline observations and interval bounds on boundary demand, what storage and OD aggregates are identifiable in a static window?
2. **Methods:** Nonnegative LP partial identification; bounded discrete conservation with separable observation and prior terms.
3. **Pilot design:** Controlled chain networks, paired seeds, stress masks and misspecified intervals; oracle boundary diagnostic separated from deployable fit.
4. **Empirical status:** Development pilot only; metrics are RMSE/coverage/width, not TrafficFlowBench composite.
5. **Open gaps before real-data claims:** Embargoed calendar adapters, ramp/boundary truth, and independent holdout evidence.
