# LaTeX preprint style provenance

This manuscript uses George Kour's **arxiv-style** preprint package, distributed on Overleaf as
[Style and Template for Preprints (arXiv, bio-arXiv)](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/style-and-template-for-preprints-arxiv-bio-arxiv/fxsnsrzpnvwc)
and maintained at [kourgeorge/arxiv-style](https://github.com/kourgeorge/arxiv-style) (MIT License).

| File | Origin |
|------|--------|
| `arxiv.sty` | Upstream `arxiv.sty` from commit on `master` (2026-11-11 fetch) |
| `third_party_arxiv_style_LICENSE.txt` | Upstream `License.txt` (MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 George Kour) |

Body text, tables, figures, and bibliography remain project-specific; only layout and title-block styling follow the upstream template.
